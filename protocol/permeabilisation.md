# The key innovation: two-step permeabilisation for tomato leaf

## Pre-treatment (novel)
| Component | Conc. | Purpose |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| PVP40 | 2% w/v | Sequesters polyphenolics |
| BSA | 0.2% w/v | Carrier protein |
| DTT | 10 mM | Breaks disulfide bonds |

Apply 100 µL · 37°C · 30 min · wash 2× 0.2× SSC

## Standard Visium enzyme
10x Genomics mix · **40 min at 37°C** (vs 12–18 min for mammalian tissue)

## QC thresholds
RIN ≥ 7.0 · Median genes/spot ≥ 500
